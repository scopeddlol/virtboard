01 / MADE FOR THE MOMENT

Less setup mid-game.
More perfect timing.

Keep the controls close and the interruptions out of your session.

⌘

A shortcut for everything.

Trigger sounds, switch pages, change voices and mute individual outputs. Global keybinds keep working while Virtboard is in the tray.

▦

A place for every sound.

Organize clips into pages. Reuse the same keys on different pages, and set each page’s default: restart, overlap or stop on a second press.

≋

Choose from 13 voice presets, or build your own with pitch, EQ, robot effects, distortion, echo, reverb and more.

⌁

Drop in your audio files, trim the timing, add fades and adjust playback speed. Preview in your headphones and export an edited WAV.

02 / CONTROL THE CONVERSATION

Talk to the squad.
Play to the lobby.

Voice Chat and Game Chat have their own microphone and soundboard mute controls, with a keybind for each. Add more output routes whenever you need them.

Each independent mix needs a different installed virtual cable device. Choose its matching recording device as the microphone in the receiving app. The global microphone mute silences your voice on all routes.

05 / READY WHEN YOU ARE

Three steps to your next session.

  1. 01

    Install Virtboard.

    Download the Windows installer here and run it. Install a virtual audio cable if you don’t already have one.

  2. 02

    Connect your chats.

    In Settings, select your real mic and a different virtual cable for each output. Select each matching cable microphone in Discord or your game.

  3. 03

    Make some noise.

    Add your clips, assign shortcuts and choose who hears your mic and sounds. Leave Game Chat’s mic muted to keep your conversation in Discord.
